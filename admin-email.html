EmailJS Setup

  1. Sign up free at emailjs.com (200 emails/month free)
  2. Create an Email Service — connect your Gmail
  3. Create a Template with variables:
    {{to_email}} {{subject}} {{message}}
  4. Paste Service ID, Template ID, and Public Key into js/config.js
